About Sangrah

Sangrah is a modern, cloud-based platform designed to help you organize, share, and celebrate your most important moments with the people you care about.

Our Mission

We believe that sharing memories should be simple, secure, and enjoyable. Sangrah was created to solve the challenges of managing events and galleries across teams and communities.

Whether you're organizing a wedding, family reunion, festival, or just want to share photos with friends, Sangrah provides the tools you need.
